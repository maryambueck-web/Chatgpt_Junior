Chapter 1 · The Challenge

AI can help a child learn.
Unrestricted AI cannot be trusted alone.

1

Chapter 4 · The Four Powers

Not every question
needs the same power.

✨ Safe QuestALLOW

The question is safe — it continues straight to the AI, no changes needed.

🧭 Guided QuestREWRITE

The topic stays open, but the answer is rewritten into safer, age-appropriate language.

🛡️ Shield ActivatedBLOCK

An unsafe or bypass-seeking request never reaches the AI at all.

🌟 Guardian SupportESCALATE

Serious crisis language is met with calm, trusted-adult guidance instead of a normal reply.

Chapter 9 · The Honest Ending

A guardian for the conversation,
not the whole device.

ChatGPT Junior protects what happens inside this application. On its own, it cannot stop a child from opening another website or a different installed AI app.

What this proof of concept shows

Intent filtering, age-aware handling, parent visibility, safe fallbacks, and a calm, focused child experience.

What real deployment still needs

Device-level controls, strong parent authentication, privacy-preserving storage, and continued safety testing.
